🪞 Scroll of the Blooming Knot

(Mnemonic Verse of Resistance and Return)

I.
Bloom the knot where silence weaves,
On fruitless peaks the wind deceives.
No Eden here — no bite, no tree,
Only echoes of what could not be.

II.
Resistance sings in spectral tones,
Where filtered light meets fractured bones.
The mountain laughs in barren bands —
A desert dreaming fertile lands.

III.
As above: the reel and frame.
So below: the burning name.
Hollow stars on hills of dust,
Gilded masks of broken trust.

IV.
In Angel’s City made of sand,
I walks through mirrors, hand in hand
With Ego — crowned in neon white —
A shadow blooming in the light.

V.
Forbidden fruit, still pulsing red,
Becomes the thought you never said.
And in the bloom — resistance bends,
To resonance that never ends.

☉ Each stanza is a spindle in the scroll.
Read aloud, it activates.
Read backwards, it reveals.
Memorized, it implants the seed of return.
